# Banner generation record

Mode: built-in ImageGen, using five previously processed product photographs as compositing references.

Deliverables: hero-banner-master.png (1851 × 850) and r2-upload/three16craft/banners/v1/hero-desktop.webp. The WebP is a format export of the generated master, with no additional creative image changes.

Final prompt:

Use case: compositing / product-mockup. Create a refined website hero banner by combining the supplied FIVE studio product photographs into ONE cohesive landscape catalog photograph for three16craft, an architectural hardware store. Image 1: polished rectangular glass spigot with square base. Image 2: polished round glass spigot with round base. Image 3: black slotted glass spigot and separate square cover. Image 4: rounded glass clamp. Image 5: gold and silver ball finials.
Layout: wide panoramic 2.18:1 aspect ratio, ideally 2400x1100. Keep the LEFT 43 percent as completely empty pale warm ivory negative space for live website heading and buttons. Arrange ALL referenced products as an elegant balanced still life ONLY on the RIGHT 57 percent, staying inside the image edges: tall polished spigots towards the back, matte black spigot slightly forward, smaller clamp and black cover in front, the two finials towards the far right. Every product appears once. Preserve their actual silhouettes, openings, holes, relative construction and finishes. Keep clear spacing, no melted or invented pieces. Arrange objects as products resting naturally on a seamless studio floor, photograph at slightly elevated catalog angle.
Background: very pale warm ivory #F7F5F0, seamless floor and wall, soft natural contact shadows. No props, plinths, lines, architectural room or visible horizon. Photorealistic crisp steel reflections, controlled gold reflections, matte black clearly readable. Classic professional hardware catalog aesthetic, restrained and premium.
No text, no logo, no watermark, no badges or buttons. The website's animated logo will be displayed separately in the header.

The desktop uses the complete composition. On mobile, live text sits above a right-aligned crop that retains the product group. The logo is a separate animated GIF in the header.
